Audit and rewrite all visible copy across the app.

Goal:
Remove AI fluff, hype, vague startup language, and over-polished generic wording. The tone should feel premium, strategic, credible, commercially sharp, and human.

Style rules:
- No em dashes.
- No emojis.
- No phrases like “unlock your potential”, “revolutionary”, “game-changing”, “supercharge”, “seamless”, “cutting-edge”, “empower”, “transform your journey”, “next-level”, “AI-powered future”, or “leverage AI to unlock”.
- Avoid inflated claims unless the feature proves them.
- Use short, direct sentences.
- Make the copy sound like it was written by a serious strategy consultant, product strategist, or elite operator.
- Prefer clarity over hype.
- Prefer specificity over broad promises.
- Make every sentence earn its place.
- Keep the tone confident but not cringe.
- Make it feel like McKinsey strategy, premium SaaS, and founder operating system combined.

Rewrite priorities:
1. Hero section
2. Feature cards
3. Dashboard labels
4. CTA buttons
5. Empty states
6. Onboarding questions
7. AI output headings
8. Portfolio/public page copy
9. Footer copy
10. Any demo/sample copy

Brand positioning:
This app is not a productivity tool.
It is a strategic operating system for people turning goals, skills, projects, and opportunities into clear execution plans.

Tone examples:
Instead of:
“Unlock your true potential with our revolutionary AI-powered strategy engine.”

Write:
“Turn unclear goals into structured decisions, ranked priorities, and executable next steps.”

Instead of:
“Supercharge your workflow with cutting-edge automation.”

Write:
“Identify where automation saves time, reduces friction, or creates commercial leverage.”

Instead of:
“Your journey to success starts here.”

Write:
“Start with the bottleneck. Then build the system around it.”

Rewrite the app using this voice:
- Precise
- Commercially aware
- Strategic
- Minimal
- Mature
- High-agency
- Calm confidence
- No motivational fluff

Also improve microcopy:
- Buttons should use action language.
- Cards should describe outcomes, not features.
- Headings should be sharp and specific.
- Empty states should guide the user, not flatter them.
- AI results should sound analytical, not inspirational.

After rewriting, scan the entire codebase for:
- em dashes
- hype phrases
- vague claims
- robotic phrasing
- repeated “AI-powered” wording
- awkward corporate language

Replace all weak copy with stronger professional wording.
Do not change app functionality unless needed to support clearer copy.
